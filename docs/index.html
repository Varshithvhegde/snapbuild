Frequently asked questions

Is Snapbuild a Lovable alternative for phone?

Yes. Snapbuild offers a similar AI chat-to-website experience as Lovable, v0, and Bolt, but with a mobile-first interface, open-source code, BYOK AI keys, and self-hosting — so you build on your own terms.

Can I build a website without coding?

Absolutely. Use templates, chat with AI, or tap elements in the preview to edit. Snapbuild is a DIY no-code website builder — code is generated for you and you can deploy with one click.

Do I need to pay for Snapbuild?

Snapbuild is free and open source (MIT). You pay only for your AI API usage, Convex free tier, and Cloudflare R2/Workers — typically pennies at small scale.

Can I deploy to my own domain?

Yes. Configure Cloudflare Worker + R2 and point your wildcard DNS. Sites publish to {slug}.yourdomain.com — full control, no platform lock-in.

How is this different from Wix or Squarespace?

Traditional DIY builders are drag-and-drop with rigid templates. Snapbuild uses AI to generate custom sites from conversation, gives you the source code, and lets you self-host — closer to Lovable/v0 than classic no-code platforms.
